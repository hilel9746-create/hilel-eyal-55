Questions Sellers Ask

Ohio Cash Offer FAQ

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. We buy houses as-is, including homes that need updates, cleanout, major repairs, or have been sitting vacant.

How fast can I close?

Many sales can close in 7-14 days, subject to clean title and the details of the property. You can also choose a later date if that works better.

Do you buy inherited homes?

Yes. We can review inherited properties and explain the next steps for a simple as-is cash offer.

Do I pay agent commissions or hidden fees?

No agent commissions are charged by us, and there is no obligation to accept the offer. The goal is a clear net number before you decide.

How do I sell my house fast in Ohio?

Call or send your address, get a written cash offer within 24 hours, and pick your closing date. Many sales close in 7–14 days, subject to clean title.

Can I sell my house without a realtor?

Yes. We are direct cash buyers, so you sell straight to us — no realtor, no listing, and no agent commissions or closing costs on your side.
