About Hillel's Team

A direct Ohio cash home buyer helping homeowners sell as-is, avoid repairs, and close with a clear, no-pressure process.

Who we are

Hillel's Team works directly with homeowners who want a simpler way to sell a house in Ohio. If the property needs repairs, has tenants, was inherited, is vacant, or simply needs to be sold quickly, we can review it and make a direct cash offer without asking you to list it, clean it out, or pay agent commissions.

You are not passed through a call center. Hilel personally reviews each situation, explains the offer, and keeps the process clear from the first call through closing.

At a glance

  • Direct Ohio Cash Buyer – We buy houses for cash and review each property based on condition, location, timing, and seller goals.
  • As-Is Sales – No repairs, cleaning, showings, open houses, or traditional listing process required.
  • Clear Timeline – Many closings can happen in 7–14 days, subject to clean title, or on a later date that works for you.
  • No Pressure – The offer is no-obligation, and you can compare it with your other selling options before deciding.

How we earn trust

We keep the process practical and transparent. You tell us about the property, we review the condition and local market, then we explain the cash offer in plain language. If the number works, we coordinate the closing. If it does not, there is no obligation.

  • Personal communication from first call to closing.
  • Written offer before you make a decision.
  • Simple explanation of timing, title, and next steps.
  • Respectful handling of sensitive situations like inheritance, foreclosure pressure, divorce, or unwanted rentals.
